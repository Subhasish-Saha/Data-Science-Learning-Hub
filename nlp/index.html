⚠ Library currency — verified August 2026

transformers 5.15.0 (10 Aug 2026) · spaCy 3.8.15 · sentence-transformers 5.7.0 · Python 3.10–3.14.

  • v5 was a breaking major. PyTorch is the only backend — TensorFlow and Flax support were removed.
  • The “slow vs fast tokenizer” distinction is gone. Saying “I'd load the slow tokenizer to inspect offsets” describes a library that no longer exists — use return_offsets_mapping and word_ids().
  • Image processors are fast/torchvision-only; attention is pluggable via AttentionInterface; quantization is a first-class loading concern; there is a built-in transformers serve plus continuous batching and paged attention.
  • Three answers in most 2024-era study material are now stale: <UNK> for OOV, sinusoidal positional encoding as the answer, and RLHF-as-PPO. The current versions are subword/byte-level tokenization, RoPE + YaRN, and SFT → DPO → GRPO/RLVR.

Every part carries a provenance note distinguishing what was verified against primary sources from what is directional. Treat any MTEB ranking or “current best model” claim as stale on sight and re-benchmark on your own data.
