About this hub

  • Adding a subject is a drop-in. Each subject lives in its own folder (e.g. rag/); a new one is just a new folder with its own index.html, plus a card added here. See README.md in the repo.
  • Progress is saved per device (each browser's local storage), so your phone and laptop track separately.
  • Any device. These are static pages — they work in any phone/tablet/desktop browser, and each subject's "Complete" page prints cleanly to PDF.
